    Fine print

    The rules.

    How prizes work
    • Reach a prize level’s points in a single run and you win that prize, as long as winners are left.
    • One prize per person for each level. Your coupon code appears on screen and under “My coupons”.
    • Coupons are valid till 31 Jan. Show the code at The Grill Cafe counter.
    The top-score prize
    • The highest single-run score recorded before 31 Dec, 9 PM (India time) wins the top-score prize.
    • Scores after that time still count toward the level prizes, but not the top-score prize.
    • The winner’s coupon is issued just after midnight, on 1 January, and shows up under “My coupons”.
    The small print
    • Winners are limited for every prize level, first come first served.
    • Please play with your own mobile number.
    • The Grill Cafe may cancel any prize and block any number if the rules aren’t followed. The cafe’s decision is final.
